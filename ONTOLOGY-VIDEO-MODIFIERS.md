# Ontology — AI Video Generation Modifiers
11 categories x 11 curated terms = 121 terms
Format: term — (effect / best use)

## MOTION-QUALITY
- buttery-smooth — (fluid motion, no jitter or stutter)
- weighty — (mass and momentum feel physically real)
- floaty — (slow gravity-defying dreamlike movement)
- snappy — (crisp accelerated cartoonish timing)
- languid — (lazy unhurried sensuous pacing)
- twitchy — (nervous erratic micro-movements)
- viscous — (thick honey-like resistance in motion)
- elastic — (springy overshoot and settle)
- glacial — (extremely slow steady inevitable motion)
- staccato — (broken rhythmic stops and starts)
- billowing — (soft rolling waves like fabric smoke)

## CAMERA-MOVE
- dolly-in — (slow push toward subject, building intimacy)
- crane-shot — (sweeping vertical rise revealing scale)
- orbit-shot — (camera circles subject, product showcase)
- tracking-shot — (lateral follow alongside moving subject)
- handheld — (organic shake, documentary realism)
- fpv-drone — (fast agile first-person flight path)
- whip-pan — (blurred fast swing between subjects)
- vertigo-zoom — (dolly-zoom combo, disorienting pull)
- low-angle-drift — (ground-level glide, heroic framing)
- snorricam — (body-locked camera, chaotic background)
- aerial-pull-back — (rising reveal from detail to landscape)

## TRANSITION
- match-cut — (seamless shape-based scene change)
- morph-cut — (subject fluidly transforms into next)
- whip-transition — (fast pan blur hides the cut)
- light-leak-wipe — (burning film flash between scenes)
- ink-bleed-reveal — (liquid ink spreads revealing scene)
- glitch-cut — (digital corruption masks the transition)
- luma-fade — (brightness-driven dissolve, soft or harsh)
- mask-wipe — (foreground object wipes frame clean)
- time-slice-transition — (frozen moment, camera passes through)
- zoom-through — (camera flies into detail, next scene)
- smoke-reveal — (parting smoke uncovers new shot)

## FRAME-RATE-FEEL
- cinematic-24fps — (classic film cadence and motion blur)
- soap-opera-60fps — (hyper-smooth broadcast-video look)
- silent-film-16fps — (charming judder of early cinema)
- anime-12fps — (limited-animation pose-to-pose feel)
- flipbook-jitter — (hand-drawn frame-to-frame wobble)
- stop-motion-8fps — (tactile clay-like stepped motion)
- slow-motion-120fps — (silky overcranked detail playback)
- timelapse-1fps — (compressed hours into seconds)
- strobing-15fps — (pulsing rave-like motion gaps)
- variable-framerate — (shifting cadence for dramatic emphasis)
- interlaced-look — (retro CRT combing artifacts)

## TIME-EFFECT
- bullet-time — (frozen subject, orbiting camera)
- speed-ramp — (slow-to-fast-to-slow dramatic shift)
- reverse-playback — (time flowing backward, unspilling)
- freeze-frame — (sudden hold on peak moment)
- time-remap-wave — (speed oscillates rhythmically)
- hyperlapse — (moving camera compresses long journeys)
- long-exposure-trails — (light and motion smear into streaks)
- shutter-drag — (syncopated blur at low shutter)
- echo-time — (repeated ghost frames trailing subject)
- slow-burn — (imperceptible gradual change over shot)
- temporal-stutter — (frames skip and repeat nervously)

## TEMPORAL-STYLE
- one-continuous-take — (unbroken shot, no visible cuts)
- dream-sequence — (soft logic-defying hazy temporality)
- memory-flashback — (desaturated grainy recollection feel)
- found-footage — (camcorder authenticity, degraded texture)
- montage-sequence — (compressed time through quick beats)

- vhs-timestamp — (retro camcorder date overlay aesthetic)
- security-cam-static — (fixed surveillance frame, flat lighting)
- livestream-drift — (unedited wandering single-camera feel)
- sports-replay — (dramatic slow-mo highlight framing)
- music-video-cut — (edit rhythm locked to imagined beat)

## PHYSICS
