# FRAME-RATE FEEL
- 24fps = cinematic standard, natural motion blur
- 30fps = broadcast, slightly smoother, video-native
- 48/60fps = hyper-real, soap-opera effect unless styled
- 12fps = stop-motion/animation cadence (see 32)
- 8fps = degraded/surveillance/dream
- ramp: 24 -> 120 for slow-mo inserts, re-time in grade
