# MOTION BLUR KINDS
- shutter-angle 180: natural film blur (24fps standard)
- long-shutter: streak blur, dream/action painting
- fast-shutter: crisp frames, strobing, documentary sports
- directional: axis-specific, wind/vehicle feel
- radial: zoom blur, speed-core, adrenaline
- smear: cartoon trail, exaggerated follow-through
- per-object: one thing blurs, rest sharp (focus tool)
