# RIG STYLES
- 2d-cutout: puppeted layers, limited motion, cheap+fast
- 2d-hand-drawn: frame-by-frame, expressive, expensive
- 3d-stylized: pixar-look, squash-friendly, render-heavy
- 3d-real: mo-cap, photographic, uncanny risk
- stop-motion: physical, tactile, time-costly, beloved
- motion-graphics: vector/typography, infographic, UI
- experimental: rotoscope, collage, mixed-media
