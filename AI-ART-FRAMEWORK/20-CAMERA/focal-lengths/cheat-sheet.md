# FOCAL LENGTH CHEAT SHEET
- 16-24mm: wide, environmental, distortion at edges, heroic scale
- 35mm: street/neutral, human context, documentary feel
- 50mm: standard, closest to human eye, flat natural
- 85mm: portrait, flattering compression, creamy bokeh
- 135mm: telephoto compression, flattened faces, paparazzi feel
- 400mm+: extreme compression, wildlife/surveillance, abstracted bg
> prompt use: "85mm portrait" > "portrait" — lens choice is a character decision
