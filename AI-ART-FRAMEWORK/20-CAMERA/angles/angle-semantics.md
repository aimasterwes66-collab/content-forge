# ANGLE SEMANTICS
- eye-level: neutral, human equal
- low-angle: power, threat, heroism, monumentality
- high-angle: vulnerability, surveillance, smallness
- dutch/tilt: unease, wrongness, instability
- bird's-eye: fate, design, abstraction, god-look
- worm's-eye: awe, vertigo, insect-scope
- over-shoulder: relationship, eavesdropping
- POV: identification, subjective truth
- canted-reverse: two unstable people, mutual distrust
